Welcome, Steward.

Hearthvale begins with 10 souls and two cottages. The town runs on one simple loop: the factory turns labour into goods, and the shop sells those goods at a markup. Your treasury pays the wages and keeps the profit.

  • Put workers in the Factory & Shop to start earning.
  • Build houses so no one is left homeless.
  • Enrol idle workers in the School — every year (30 days) they gain a level of education, and skilled workers produce far more.
  • Hit milestones to unlock the Bank and Community College.

One in-game day passes each real minute. Use the speed buttons up top to fast-forward the years.
