Welcome, Steward.

Tawapur runs entirely on rupees (₹), and your treasury is funded by one thing only — tax. You spend it on houses and new buildings.

  • 18% indirect tax on every good sold at the Shop.
  • Income tax on educated workers — 12% at Basic, rising with each level.
  • The Factory makes goods; people buy them with their wages.
  • Every 5 days a census runs: with enough goods and housing, two babies are born — if either falls short, a villager dies.
  • Educate workers for more output, higher pay, and more tax. Reach 5 Intermediate workers to build the College.

One day passes each real minute. Fast-forward with the speed buttons above.
